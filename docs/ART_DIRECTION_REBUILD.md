# Art direction rebuild — 2026-10-27

Supersedes the pale sage/cream attempt in SOFT_BOSSES_AND_TRACKS.md. Runtime art now uses a new set of modular bosses, background panels, and painted ground/drive components.

## Direction and saved assets

The unchanged pink heroine was the character reference. Bosses use large cel-shaded teal armor planes, ochre honey/brass trim, warm dark contours and small cocoa faces. Walker, boiler cannon, boat and tank have distinct silhouettes. Environments show a working town, industrial factory, harbor and low military bunkers; distance is softer and the lower play area stays relatively open. Repetitive procedural midground walls/fences were removed.

- assets/generated/bosses/{walker,cannon,ship,tank}_ink.png — 32 registered body/weapon/hatch/debris sprites.
- assets/generated/bosses/drive_parts.png — transparent painted wheel and tread components.
- assets/generated/zones_{town,factory,harbor,fortress}_ink.png — 24 scenery panels.
- assets/generated/ground_ink.png — four stage-specific ground lips aligned to FLOOR.

All art was generated/edited with the built-in image_gen tool, then copied into this workspace. No CLI/API fallback, programmatic recoloring, or replacement vector artwork was used. Alpha-bound registration crops sprites at draw time; the generated files retain their transparency. Walker and boat layouts were revised to separate adjacent parts. Historical *_soft.png and *_stable.png files are retained but not loaded for current bosses.

## Runtime integration

web/render.js and scripts/main.gd load the same assets and use matching source/destination metadata in web/visuals.js and scripts/visual_motion.gd. The historical SOFT_BOSSES constant name remains for compatibility, but now registers *_ink.png. tools/inspect-soft-bosses.ps1 defaults to the ink variant.

Painted shoes move across the front and lower/rear visible belt; painted wheels rotate. Motion uses signed ground displacement, stops when parked/airborne/dead, and reverses with the vehicle. The intro tank uses the same art and track helper. Body damage, detachable guns, recoil and wreck collapse remain connected to existing states and hit boxes.
